# DueFlow
DueFlow is an invoice management web application that helps businesses track invoices, manage payments, and follow up with customers who haven't paid.

### What DueFlow does

- Dashboard: Shows total outstanding payments, overdue invoices, invoices due today, and money collected.
- Invoice Management: Add, edit, view, and delete invoices.
- Payment Tracking: Record full or partial payments and view payment history.
- Overdue Tracking: Identify unpaid invoices and monitor their due dates.
- Customer Management: Organize customer details and contact information.
- Follow-up Reminders: Identify customers who need payment reminders and open WhatsApp to contact them.
- Analytics: View invoice and payment summaries to understand business performance.
- CSV Export: Export invoice data for use in Excel or other tools.
- Dark Mode: Switch between light and dark themes.

In simple words: DueFlow helps business owners know who owes them money, how much is pending, which payments are overdue, and whom they need to contact.

DueFlow is a web-based application designed to provide a simple and intuitive user experience through a clean interface and interactive functionality.
